## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

GigShield is a dual-portal fintech web application — a portable benefit wallet for India's gig economy. It serves two distinct user types: gig delivery workers (Raju Yadav, 28, Bengaluru, earning Rs 24,200/month across Swiggy, Blinkit, and Ola) on a mobile-first worker portal, and platform compliance managers (Priya Menon, 34, Swiggy HQ) on a desktop-first admin panel. The product aggregates welfare contributions from multiple gig platforms into a single worker-owned wallet, unlocking insurance, pension, and formal credit. This is a judge-facing competition prototype — every screen must communicate regulatory credibility and product completeness at a glance.
